Microservices Book Demo - Architecture

- Product Service
  - Responsibilities: Manages catalog of products available for purchase.
  - Communicates with: Cart Service, Order Service.

- Cart Service
  - Responsibilities: Handles shopping cart operations for users.
  - Communicates with: Product Service, Order Service.

- Order Service
  - Responsibilities: Creates and manages customer orders.
  - Communicates with: Cart Service, Payment Service, Delivery Service.

- Payment Service
  - Responsibilities: Processes payments for completed orders.
  - Communicates with: Order Service.

- Delivery Service
  - Responsibilities: Schedules and tracks delivery of orders.
  - Communicates with: Order Service.

- Customer Service
  - Responsibilities: Manages customer registration, login, and profiles.
  - Communicates with: Auth Service, Order Service.

- Auth Service
  - Responsibilities: Handles authentication using JWT tokens.
  - Communicates with: Customer Service.
